# OpenFisca evaluation (not installed)

Before adoption, verify:

- Supported income-tax and resident-tax years and formula precision.
- Coverage of municipality-specific national health insurance and insurer-specific dependent eligibility.
- Multiple employers, transport allowance, partial-year coverage and temporal inputs.
- Result explanations, missing-input behavior, rounding and reproducibility with pinned engine/rule versions.
- Independent official examples and cost of maintaining missing rules versus a small implementation.
- License obligations before copying or integrating code.

Do not send personal inputs to a public demonstration service. Use synthetic examples locally. Keep the input/output adapter separate from application storage and UI.
